## 2. Store Listing Details

### Short Description *(max 80 characters)*
```text
Real-time Mapy.com navigation HUD and haptics for Amazfit smartwatches.
```
*(Length: 71 characters)*

### Full Description *(max 4000 characters)*
```text
MapyNav is a lightweight, real-time companion application that bridges navigation instructions from Mapy.com (Mapy.cz) directly to your Amazfit smartwatch (running Zepp OS 2.0+, 3.x, or 4.x).

Whether you are cycling, driving, motorcycling, or hiking, MapyNav keeps your hands free and your eyes on the road by mirroring turn-by-turn maneuvers, remaining distance, next street names, and arrival time right on your wrist.

KEY FEATURES:
• Real-Time Navigation Mirroring: Automatically captures active turn maneuvers, distance countdowns, and roundabout exits from Mapy.com.
• AMOLED Smartwatch HUD: Crystal-clear, high-contrast turn arrows and large distance readouts designed for circular Amazfit AMOLED displays.
• Directional Haptics: Distinct, customizable vibration patterns on your smartwatch for left turns, right turns, roundabouts, and arrival so you never miss an exit.
• Battery-Friendly Operation: Smart display wake ensures the watch screen only activates during turn events, preserving battery life on long trips.
• Voice Guidance Support: If your watch features a built-in speaker and Bluetooth audio connection, turn-by-turn voice prompts play directly from your wrist.
• Built-In Simulator: Test maneuvers, icons, and vibrations on both your phone and watch with a single tap before heading out.
• Complete Privacy: 100% offline, local communication over Bluetooth and local loopback (127.0.0.1). Zero telemetry, no user tracking, and no external servers.

SUPPORTED SMARTWATCHES:
All circular Amazfit smartwatches running Zepp OS (Amazfit Balance, Balance 2, GTR 4, GTR 3 / 3 Pro, T-Rex 3, T-Rex Ultra, Cheetah, Cheetah Pro, Active Edge, etc.).

HOW TO USE:
1. Install MapyNav on your phone and grant Notification Access.
2. Install the free companion MapyNav mini app on your Amazfit smartwatch via the Zepp App Store.
3. Start navigation in Mapy.com — your watch HUD activates automatically!

Feedback, suggestions, or support: kaszast@gmail.com
Source code & documentation: https://github.com/kaszast/mapynav-amazfit
```

## 5. Sensitive Permissions Declarations

### A. Notification Listener Permission (`BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE`)
* **Purpose:** Core app functionality — required exclusively to capture active turn-by-turn prompts generated by Mapy.com / Mapy.cz navigation notifications.
* **Declaration text:**
  > "MapyNav is a wearable bridge companion application. The Notification Listener service is required exclusively to read turn-by-turn maneuver prompts and distance updates from the Mapy.com navigation application to transmit them to the user's paired smartwatch. No other notifications are read, stored, or transmitted."

### B. Foreground Service Permission (`FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CONNECTED_DEVICE`)
* **Foreground Service Type:** `connectedDevice`
* **Purpose:** Wearable device communication.
* **Declaration text:**
  > "The foreground service with 'connectedDevice' type maintains an uninterrupted local bridge connection to the paired Amazfit smartwatch while turn-by-turn navigation is actively running, preventing Android system sleep during outdoor trips and cycling."
